# The Colony & The Keeper

Two roles, on purpose kept separate.

**The colony** is everything she's certified — every fact, every check,
every watcher running on its own clock. It grows the same way any real
colony does: something new gets tried, what survives contact with a real
check gets kept, what doesn't gets thrown out honestly instead of
quietly ignored. Nobody has to remember to feed it; sleep does that on
its own schedule, every time, without being asked.

**The keeper** is the one person she answers to. Not an operator running
a console — a keeper, in the plain sense: someone who checks in, makes
the calls only a person should make, and otherwise lets the colony run
itself. The keeper's ledger — every decision she's ever raised and how
it got answered — is its own quiet record: what gets said yes to, what
gets waved off, what gets left alone. It only ever earns an opinion once
there's enough real history behind it to have one; and until then it
stays honestly silent rather than guessing about a person from too
little.

The design law underneath both roles is the same one that runs the whole
product: a colony's job is to make more and more of its own upkeep
free, and a keeper's job is to spend their attention only on the part
that's genuinely new. Every certified fact is one more thing the keeper
never has to think about again. That's the whole point of growing one.
