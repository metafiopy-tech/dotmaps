# How She Learns

Three plain steps, every time, in this order — never skipped, never
reordered:

**1. She grows a guess.** Handed something new, she pokes at it — reads a
file, tries a fetch, looks around — and drafts a claim about what she
found: *this file always has exactly five items in it*, say. A claim on
its own is worth nothing; the next step is what makes it real.

**2. She banks it, only if it can fail.** Before a claim is allowed to
count as learned, it has to survive one honest test: replayed against a
clean copy of the same world, does it hold? And replayed against a
*deliberately broken* copy — files emptied, text stripped — does it
correctly fall apart? A claim that says "true" no matter what it's shown
isn't a check, it's decoration, and it gets thrown out right here, before
it ever reaches her memory. Only a claim that can be wrong, and knows
when it's wrong, gets banked.

**3. She certifies it, on a schedule, without being asked.** A banked
claim starts out unproven — a candidate, not yet trusted. On her own
clock (no ask required), she replays it enough times, against enough
copies of the world, to earn real statistical confidence that it holds.
Once it clears that bar, it graduates: certified. From that moment on,
anything that asks the same question gets the certified answer back
instantly, for nothing — no model, no waiting, no cost.

That clock never really stops. A certified thing that hasn't been used
in a while gets quietly re-checked before it's trusted again — the same
way a fact you haven't thought about in months gets a second look before
you'd swear to it. Nothing certified is certified forever on faith; it's
certified until the next scheduled look, always.

This is the whole mechanism behind the two chips you see on every
message: the free one only exists because something, at some point, went
through all three of these steps for real.

**receipts:** the confirm/discriminate gate and the certify-then-decay
clock are both live code, not a diagram — every card in her memory names
the run that banked it and the check that certified it.
