# QUEEN v0 — The Body
## Mined from the vault: you already designed her twice

The archive shows the queen was designed on July 16 (the symbiotic team —
five mutualists, oracle-checked) and July 23 (the swarm executive — hears
only trips, never polls). This spec fuses those two designs with what the
52-run campaign since then *proved*, and marks every component: MINED
(from vault), PROVEN (has data), or OPEN (genuinely undesigned).

---

## 1 · What the queen is NOT (settled by data, not taste)

- NOT a cost optimizer. The efficiency claim is DEAD, permanently, by its
  own final-trial clause. A queen that dispatches to save money is
  dispatching on a falsified premise. [PROVEN — e1d-verdict]
- NOT a manager. "The giraffe never runs a standup with the bird" — the
  moment dispatch requires supervision, coordination tax eats the value.
  [MINED — symbiotic session]
- NOT a crew. She makes room for the real thing; she doesn't stand in for
  it. Two ledgers, kept separate. [MINED — symbiotic session, verbatim]
- NOT an advisor to her agents. Advice loses to gates: informational
  surfacing went 5/10, mechanical gates went 36/36 and 0/16. Every queen
  rule is a door, never a suggestion. [PROVEN — E1b/E1c/E1d]

## 2 · What the queen dispatches FOR (the surviving claims)

- **Frontier depth.** Inheritance makes agents 12× deeper adversarial
  explorers (63 vs 5 mutation rules, 12/13 arms). The queen's core move:
  equip an agent with certified skills and aim it at the manifest's
  `frontier` list — the machine-readable frontier of incompetence (H1,
  operational). [PROVEN — e1d-verdict + EQUIP §2.4]
- **Free coverage.** Certified maps traverse at $0, zero model calls. The
  queen never spends a model on covered ground. [PROVEN — G3]
- **Friction metabolism.** The five mutualist niches, each eating one
  class of Fio's waste: Groundskeeper (cold-start state), Scribe
  (provenance under flow), Forager (opportunity firehose), Composter
  (skill extraction from finished work — this is BANK, already built),
  Gatekeeper (the anti-parasite oracle). [MINED — symbiotic session]

## 3 · Architecture (assembly of certified parts)

### 3.1 Routing — manifest coverage, nothing else
Task arrives → compiler (frozen T2 rubric, exists) → predicates → per
predicate: covered → certified skill executes on cheapest certified rung;
frontier → equip-and-grow dispatch. Exact-match routing; auditable; no
semantic vibes. [PROVEN — G3 route.py, both directions demonstrated]

### 3.2 Hearing — sparse signals only
The queen never polls the colony. She hears TRIPS: certification events,
convictions, gate blocks, budget exhaustion, covenant breaks. Everything
else is silence. [MINED — swarm session: "the executive only hears what
trips, never polls"]

### 3.3 The four queen functions (from EQUIP spec, unchanged)
READ (compile) · STAFF (assemble insect from certified skills — genes not
bodies, §2.5) · BUDGET (H1: retire at certification, redeploy to
frontier; per-skill θ from stakes) · ESCALATE (trip log to Fio; embodied
trigger — a state he's in, not a command he issues). [MINED + PROVEN]

### 3.4 The Gatekeeper is not optional
Every mutualist is oracle-checked or it rots into parasitism — "an agent
optimized on tickets closed will create tickets." The Gatekeeper runs the
same machinery as skill certification: does this agent's output still
verify against ground truth OUTSIDE the system? A queen without a
Gatekeeper decays to the STARVED fixed point: confident, coherent,
ungrounded. [MINED — symbiotic session; mechanism PROVEN — oracle gate]

### 3.5 Establishment protocol — one mutualist at a time
Symbioses establish serially, each proven net-positive before the next
attaches. Queen v0 launches with ONE agent under dispatch (the
Composter/BANK — already built and certified) and adds the second only
after the first survives its own Gatekeeper audit. [MINED — symbiotic
session; anti-Voyager discipline PROVEN — R1 research]


### 3.6 The queen has a NATURE, not a skin [MINED — character framework, March]
"Character as load-bearing structure": everyone else applies personality as
paint; in your stack the character IS the mechanism — the incompleteness
loop doesn't make the system feel alive, it's why it keeps going. The
queen's character is her interface physics: sparse, trip-only, never
nagging, triggered by states Fio is already in. **Container precedes
content** — her shape changes what Fio brings to her. She is an entity,
not an assistant, and the March session's warning applies: her depth comes
from accumulated time-in-service, not capability. (One more argument for
serial establishment — she gets deep by being there, not by shipping big.)

### 3.7 The theory-of-Fio organ — OPEN HOLE #1 HAS A MINED ANSWER
March built "Purple": a component that watches which of two responses Fio
actually chooses and develops a theory of his cognitive signature over
time. That IS the adaptive trip threshold: the queen logs every escalation
and whether Fio acted, deferred, or ignored — and the threshold learns
from the ledger. Not designed from scratch; excavated. First version can
be dumb (act-rate per trip category, mechanical cutoff — gates not advice,
per the law).

### 3.8 The homeostasis organ [MINED — maintenance session, March]

### 3.9 Fracture-location over resolution [MINED — character framework]

## 4a · HOLE-CLOSURE PASS — the answers were in the mining session

All three holes close from one source: the brain-exercise session
(2026-07-28/29), where the abort criteria were excavated from the golf
gig, the .400 season, the relationship, and the creative-work spiral.

### Hole 1 CLOSED — the Gatekeeper's net-positive metric [MINED]
The golf-gig oracle-validity tells ARE the mutualist audit, inverted onto
the agent. A mutualist is net-positive iff, on a pre-registered cadence:
1. **State-change test** — its outputs produce measurable state change in
   the host (act-rate from Purple's ledger: did Fio's behavior differ
   because the brief existed?). "Prior output produced no state change in
   him" was the golf-gig kill tell; it kills mutualists the same way.
2. **Transfer test** — "off-objective return is only real if it transfers
   to a domain that didn't generate it." The Groundskeeper's value must
   show up as changed decisions OUTSIDE its own consumption loop, or it's
   narrative.
3. **Parasite tell** — "an agent optimized on tickets closed will create
   tickets." Audit whether the friction class the agent eats is growing
   under its stewardship. An oxpecker keeping the wound open fails here.
Fail any one → the mutualist demotes to candidate and re-earns.

### Hole 2 CLOSED — dispatch under variance [MINED + PROVEN]
Four rules, ordered:
1. **Oracle first, always** — "from the inside a broken oracle and a real
   ceiling look identical." No policy update until the evaluator passes
   structural validity (shows up / updates on output / approval coupled to
   action / silence informative). Retire the evaluator, never the skill,
   on oracle failure. [G1 ordering, now with its origin story]
2. **Trajectory verdicts, never single runs** — policy updates require the
   typed-category wall/churn/directional read against the chi-null, not
   one outcome. (52 runs of variance data say single-run updates are
   noise-chasing. [PROVEN])
3. **Off-objective ledger** — runs that fail their objective may pay on
   unmeasured axes (eq-01 "failed" at 20u while teaching us the gate
   works). The dispatch ledger records transfer returns so the queen
   doesn't bin the presentation skills with the marketing gig.
4. **Provenance guard** — the third abort criterion reads WHOSE objective
   it is, and fired at .400 while every reward said continue. The queen
   never updates policy on returns from self-generated objectives —
   the first-person mesa-optimization guard, from lived data.

### Hole 3 CLOSED — the abort governor, complete anatomy [MINED]
Three orthogonal criteria plus a counterweight, each from a different
shaft of the same session:
- **Reads the skill:** competence-flatness via typed failure categories +
  chi-null churn test (H9 — the baseball body-knowledge shaft).
- **Reads the evaluator:** oracle-validity structural tells, cheap and
  early, fires in ~3 interactions where flatness needs a budget burned
  (the golf-gig shaft).
- **Reads the objective:** provenance — is this mine? — capable of firing
  while performance is intact (the quit-at-.400 shaft; candidate novelty:
  first-person goal-misgeneralization detection, flagged for lit pass).
- **The counterweight:** the persistence budget — internally generated,
  spent independent of returns ("confidence is the greatest validator
  because it isn't one"; the creative-work data point: persistence at
  zero-to-negative signal). Without it, three abort criteria make the
  colony a quitting machine. Budget sizing is the one constant that
  needs data, not design.
